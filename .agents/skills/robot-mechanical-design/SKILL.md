# Robot mechanical design

Start from verified rules, measured components, available tools, materials, and fabrication time. Never invent dimensions or load ratings.

- Define the mechanism objective, interfaces, load cases, motion envelope, failure modes, and inspection method.
- Use SI units in calculations and state display units explicitly.
- Show assumptions, safety factors, tolerances, fasteners, material, mass estimate, and fabrication sequence.
- Prefer adjustable, reversible, easily fabricated concepts during competition time.
- Check collisions, reach, ground clearance, center of mass, wire routing, assembly access, and serviceability.
- Record released envelopes and mounting patterns in `shared/INTERFACES.md`.
- Export or attach a dimensioned drawing, CAD preview, BOM, and human-verification checklist before handoff.

An agent may generate CAD or calculations, but a human must verify measurements, fit, fabrication setup, and physical safety.
